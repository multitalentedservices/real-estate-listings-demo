About

A small agency, presented honestly

Arz Estates is a fictional estate agency created for this portfolio demonstration. It does not exist, holds no listings and cannot sell or rent you anything. What is real is the website itself: the search, filters, property pages and enquiry paths all work exactly as they would for a genuine agency.

The demo catalogue spans four cities — Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Gujranwala — and three property types: houses, apartments and plots, for sale and for rent. Prices are shown the way Pakistani buyers actually read them, in lakh and crore, with sizes in marla, kanal and square feet.

What a real version would add

  • Verified listings with agent-confirmed availability
  • Real photography and floor plans per property
  • A live map with the exact plot or unit location
  • Saved-search alerts by email and WhatsApp
